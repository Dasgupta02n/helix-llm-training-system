Account

Your Helix account

An account is how Helix keeps your profile and your data — not a chat session. Sign in from the studio to create a profile, store gold, and export files you own.

Named profile Data stays in your library Export anytime No card on file

What you can do

  • Create a profile — name, work email, password. Change your name and password after you sign in.
  • Store data — gold examples, materials, synthesized rows, and job history live in your account library until you archive or delete them.
  • Use a workspace — each account gets a workspace so plans and libraries stay scoped to you.
  • Export — download gold, materials, or a trainable package from My data and Download.
  • Train later — Double Helix fetches gold already in this account. You do not re-upload.

How access works (beta)

  1. Open the studio and choose Create account.
  2. Confirm your email if verification is enabled.
  3. Wait for admin approval if that gate is on. You get an email when the account is activated.
  4. Sign in. Open Account in the studio to edit your profile and see what is stored.

Helix does not take payments. There is no checkout and no card data on this site. See Pricing and Privacy.

What we store for you

  • Account fields: email, display name, password hash, verification and approval flags.
  • Workspace content you create: research plan, gold, synthesized rows, uploaded zips, job logs.
  • If you accept a trained-model declaration, that copy is emailed and kept (it cannot be deleted).

You can delete the account from the studio Account tab. Email us if you need help: dasgupta.02n@gmail.com.
