Break the hire-or-stall bind without replacing the person

Working capital is already in cloth, receivables, or an OEM’s sixty-day cycle. You cannot hire five people until the books allow it. You cannot grow the books until someone takes the extra returns, invoices, and calls. C7X does not replace that someone. It gives them specialist workers trained on how they already decide.

The three cases are composites from Tiruppur, Pune, and Hyderabad — not named logos. On a high-volume written desk, one trusted person plus a specialist brain is the example of a five-person bench. The first month is still that person training a junior. After that, they keep the judgment, the cash, and the relationship.

Tiruppur knitwear returns

Tamil Nadu · export apparel

A 120-machine knitwear exporter in Tiruppur ships basic T-shirts and hoodies to US and EU buyers. The floor is fast. The bottleneck is the WhatsApp returns desk: every damaged carton lands on one supervisor who already walks the line. She is not the person you replace. She is the person you staff.

The job as it is done today

Buyers and buying-house QC send photos and a one-line complaint. The desk must classify the defect (stitching, shade, hole, size, late ship), decide refund / remake / reject, calculate the INR amount against the contract, flag a repeat supplier, and write a Tamil + English note the floor can act on before the next cutting ticket.

What C7X trains

  • Gold rows from last season’s settled returns — photo description, contract clause, the decision the supervisor actually took, and why.
  • Edge cases Riu will not skip: a buyer who always claims shade variation, a carton damaged by the liner, a style that was already closed.
  • A refusal: do not invent a credit note when the PO number is missing. Ask one clarifying question, then hand off.

What changes on the floor

The specialist brain drafts the classification and the bilingual floor note. She reviews exceptions and signs credits — the example of a five-person WhatsApp bench, on the one person the floor already trusts. She walks the line again. Inference runs on a small PC in the cabin with Ollama. Buyer photos never go to a hosted chat box, and growth is not another five CTC in working capital.

Pune auto-component GST coding

Maharashtra · ancillary manufacturing

A Pune ancillary that machines housings for two-wheeler OEMs receives 80–150 vendor invoices a day. Two clerks type HSN, GST rate, and GL into the books. Month-end is a pile. Input-tax-credit mistakes show up as notices, not as a software error.

The job as it is done today

Each bill is a scan or a WhatsApp photo. The clerk matches the vendor, proposes an HSN from a worn cheat-sheet, checks whether ITC is allowed on that line, and posts. Anything odd — a freight line, a reverse-charge entry, a vendor who changed GSTIN — waits for the senior accountant.

What C7X trains

  • Gold from posted invoices the senior already signed: vendor, line text, HSN, rate, ITC yes/no, GL, and the one-line reason.
  • Edge cases: capital goods vs consumable, mixed credit notes, e-way bill mismatch, a SEZ supply that looks domestic.
  • A hard boundary: never file GSTR-1 or GSTR-3B. Propose the coding. A person posts.

What changes on the floor

The specialist brain reads the bill and returns a coding slip. Clear lines post after a glance. Exceptions stay with the senior. The same two people clear a day that used to spill into Saturday — the hire the working capital could not stand. Papers stay on the office PC. See which finance desks a custom LLM can staff.

Hyderabad diagnostic lab front desk

Telangana · neighbourhood diagnostics

A neighbourhood lab in Hyderabad runs blood tests, ECG, and ultrasound. The phone never stops: is fasting required, when is the report ready, can a home collection come to LB Nagar, what does this abbreviation on the slip mean. The receptionist is also taking cash and handing reports.

The job as it is done today

Every answer already exists in a printed rate list and a fasting chart taped to the wall. The cost is repetition, in Telugu and English, while a queue watches. Wrong fasting advice means a redraw. A late report call that could have been “after 6 pm” becomes an argument at the counter.

What C7X trains

  • Gold from the actual rate list, fasting chart, and a week of the questions the desk already answered well.
  • Bilingual output: Telugu or English, matching the patient. No diagnosis. No “your result means…”.
  • A refusal: anything that interprets a value, recommends a medicine, or guesses a missing patient ID is handed to the technician or the reporting doctor.

What changes on the floor

WhatsApp and the front-desk terminal draft the answer the chart already holds. She confirms, takes payment, and has time for the person in the chair. Reports stay on the clinic machine. A custom LLM does not replace the pathologist or the receptionist. It stops her desk from being a call centre she cannot staff.

How an MSME copies this

One role at a time
  1. Pick one desk that already makes the same decision all day.
  2. Talk to Riu in plain English. Give one perfect example and the edge cases.
  3. Upload last month’s settled tickets, bills, or FAQs — or start from a 10-row review.
  4. Export the gold library. Confirm C7X-IO if you want a QLoRA adapter.
  5. Load the adapter on a shop PC with Ollama, Open Interpreter, or the PEFT script.
