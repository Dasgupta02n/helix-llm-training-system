About C7X

C7X is a training-data studio for product and operations teams: mine domain-specific gold examples, quality-gate them, synthesize variants, and export fine-tune-ready datasets—without a labeling factory.

Who operates this site

c7xai.in is operated by Sabyasachi Dasgupta as an early-stage product (c7x AI / C7X). Primary contact: dasgupta.02n@gmail.com.

This is a real multi-tenant product in active development—not a static marketing shell. The public beta runs mining jobs, libraries, and exports for signed-in workspaces.

What we are (and are not)

  • We are a dataset mining + curation studio for task-specific LLM training data.
  • We are not a general chatbot, a hosted foundation-model provider, or primarily a prompt/eval console (those tools solve a different job).
