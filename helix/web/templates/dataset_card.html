Trust · dataset card

Dataset and adapter card

Eight sections a reviewer expects on an AI artefact. Empty claims are written as not claimed, not omitted. Early beta. No SOC 2.

Sample gold Trust center

1. Purpose

One-role specialist worker for a desk that already makes the same decision all day. C7X does not replace that person. It staffs them. Not a general chatbot and not a hosted foundation model.

2. Training data

Gold rows the account owns: interview examples, uploaded pairs, mined-and-gated evidence. Synthetics are stored in a separate file and join training only after you confirm. The public sample JSONL is an operator-authored composite, not customer data.

3. Base

Apache-2.0 or MIT instruct checkpoints only, up to 30B. Default: Qwen2.5 7B Instruct. You must have a license to the base you serve. C7X does not ship Llama weights.

4. Method

QLoRA adapter. The zip is qlora/, tokenizer, meta.json (base_model + license), gold used, and load_adapter.py. Not a merged 7B–30B dump. Not a rented endpoint.

5. Metrics

Not claimed. C7X does not publish a public leaderboard or a single accuracy number for every desk. You eval the worker on the PC that will run it.

6. Limits

Riu collects refusals before mining (missing PO, diagnosis, licensed sign-off). The worker must not invent a credit note, a medicine, or a filing. Beta software can fail. Do not store highly regulated personal data here yet.

7. Human oversight

The person at the desk samples, posts, or refuses. Licensed acts (CA, pathologist, advocate) stay human. A custom LLM is not a licence.

8. Deployment

Weights leave with you. Serve with Ollama, Open Interpreter, LM Studio, llama.cpp, or the PEFT script. C7X does not host production inference. If the shop PC is off, the worker is off.
