Roles · harness Staff the person · do not replace them

Give one person specialist workers — not a replacement

A custom-trained LLM in a local harness is not a general employee and not a hosted chatbot. It is one role, written into weights, sitting next to the person who already knows the job. Some queues it can run while they audit a sample. Some it only drafts. A few it must refuse.

Written for Indian MSME desks that cannot float a five-person hire. Pair it with the Tiruppur, Pune, and Hyderabad cases and the Ollama / Open Interpreter serving guide.

Three ways the specialist sits beside the person

Staff · draft · multiply
Staff the queue

The person audits a sample

The decision is bounded, frequent, and already written down. The specialist brain classifies, replies, and logs. The human samples the day — they are not deleted from it.

First pass

The person still posts

The worker drafts the coding, the screen, or the letter. Someone with authority accepts, edits, or sends it back. Volume still drops sharply.

Example: five

The expert covers a bench they could not hire

The senior still decides the hard cases. They stop answering the same forty questions. On a typical desk, one person plus the worker is the example of five — not a guarantee.

What stays with the human — always

Train as a refusal
  • Licensed sign-off: CA authentication, medical diagnosis or prescription, an advocate’s filing.
  • Cash, inventory movement, and anything that needs a pair of hands on the floor.
  • Statutory notices, show-cause replies, and union communication.
  • Any case where the gold library has no example and the boundary says “ask a person”.

Riu collects that boundary before mining starts. A worker that does not know when to stop is not a worker you can put on an MSME desk.

Why the harness matters

Weights + tools

The custom-trained LLM holds the judgment. The harness is how that judgment touches the shop:

  • Ollama — a named local model the desk can type into, or call from a small internal page.
  • Open Interpreter — the same model, allowed to touch files and scripts on that PC (rename a scan, fill a CSV, open today’s folder).
  • LM Studio or llama.cpp — a local OpenAI-compatible server for tools you already wrote.
  • Your own thin app — a WhatsApp Business hook or a Tally-adjacent form that posts to localhost and writes a decision log.

Without a harness the worker is a chat window. With one, the returns bench you could not hire sits next to the reviewer who already knows last season’s clause. That is the staffing story the case studies describe — five as an example, the human as the customer.
