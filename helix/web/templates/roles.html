Roles a custom-trained LLM can hold — fully, partly, or manyfold faster

A custom-trained LLM in a local harness is not a general employee and not a hosted chatbot. It is one role, written into weights, sitting next to the person who already knows the job. Some queues it can take end to end. Some it only drafts. A few it must refuse.

The list below is written for Indian MSME and ops teams. Pair it with the Tiruppur, Pune, and Hyderabad cases and the Ollama / Open Interpreter serving guide.

Three ways the worker sits on a desk

Replace · assist · multiply
Full take

The queue disappears

The decision is bounded, frequent, and already written down. The worker classifies, replies, and logs. A person audits a sample, not every row.

Partial

First pass, human posts

The worker drafts the coding, the screen, or the letter. Someone with authority accepts, edits, or sends it back. Volume still drops sharply.

Multiplier

The expert covers manyfold more

The senior still decides the hard cases. They stop answering the same forty questions. One supervisor does the work that used to need a bench.

Roles that can run end to end

High volume · written rules
Full take

WhatsApp / email L1 desk

Order status, size chart, courier tracking, store hours. The script already exists. The worker answers in the shop’s language and hands off when the order id is missing.

Full take

Returns and defect classification

Stitch, shade, hole, size, late ship. Refund, remake, or reject. The Tiruppur knitwear desk is this job.

Full take

IT helpdesk L1

Password reset steps, VPN, printer, “have you tried the other browser”. Anything that needs an admin token is a hand-off.

Full take

Lab and clinic FAQ

Fasting, report-ready time, home collection, what a code on the slip means. No interpretation of a result. The Hyderabad front desk is this job.

Full take

HR leave and policy Q&A

Casual leave, PF, notice period, holiday roster — answers the handbook already contains. Approvals stay with the manager.

Full take

Catalog copy from a spec sheet

Turn a measurement table into a marketplace listing in a fixed brand voice. A merchandiser spot-checks new styles.

Roles that stay a first pass

Person still posts
Partial

GST / AP invoice coding

HSN, rate, ITC, GL. The worker proposes; the accountant posts. See the Pune auto-component case.

Partial

Claims and warranty first review

Read the form, pull the matching policy line, recommend pay / deny / ask. A person signs the letter.

Partial

Inbound CV screen

Match a JD the company wrote. Shortlist and a one-line reason. No hire / no-hire as a final act.

Partial

Purchase-order matching

Three-way match of PO, GRN, and bill. Exceptions (qty, rate, missing e-way) go to purchase.

Partial

NCR and quality write-up

Turn a floor note and a photo description into the plant’s NCR template. QA still closes the loop.

Partial

Dealer or branch briefing

Draft the day’s price list reply or scheme FAQ. The area manager sends it.

First draft of a working paper or a letter from the firm’s own gold. The professional still signs. The licence is not the model’s.

What a custom LLM must not fully replace

Train as a refusal
  • Licensed sign-off: CA authentication, medical diagnosis or prescription, an advocate’s filing.
  • Cash, inventory movement, and anything that needs a pair of hands on the floor.
  • Statutory notices, show-cause replies, and union communication.
  • Any case where the gold library has no example and the boundary says “ask a person”.

Riu collects that boundary before mining starts. A worker that does not know when to stop is not a worker you can put on an MSME desk.

Why the harness matters

Weights + tools

The custom-trained LLM holds the judgment. The harness is how that judgment touches the shop:

  • Ollama — a named local model the desk can type into, or call from a small internal page.
  • Open Interpreter — the same model, allowed to touch files and scripts on that PC (rename a scan, fill a CSV, open today’s folder).
  • LM Studio or llama.cpp — a local OpenAI-compatible server for tools you already wrote.
  • Your own thin app — a WhatsApp Business hook or a Tally-adjacent form that posts to localhost and writes a decision log.

Without a harness the worker is a chat window. With one, a six-person returns bench becomes one reviewer plus a model that never forgets last season’s clause. That is the efficiency gain the case studies describe.
