Meet Riu

Riu is your studio guide—not another chatbot to train

Riu (pronounced ree-oo) is Helix’s conversational operator. She does not answer customer tickets. She interviews you, configures the studio, estimates cost, and starts mining only after you say so.

R

Who she is

A plain-English setup agent inside Helix. No jargon. One or two questions at a time. She remembers your plan, formats, goals, and uploads across the session.

When work starts, mining jobs keep running after you log out. Riu is the front door—not the model you ship.

What she asks

  1. What role will the AI perform?
  2. One perfect example, then 1–3 edge cases (more if the role is high-risk).
  3. Official per-row estimate. Confirm — then mining starts.
  4. After gold is ready: optional variations (you opt in).
Example exchange

You: Screen CVs for a backend role.

Riu: High-risk hiring role — I need 3 edge cases and a human-review quality bar. Give me one perfect score request and ideal reply.

You: I have no corpus. I want 5000 gold.

Riu: 5,000 gold with your sources is about $3,750–$5,000. Web-only starts at 10 examples (~$20–$30), then review. Type start 10.

SEO · GEO

Gold data that wins search and generative answers

SEO is how people find you in search. GEO (generative engine optimization) is how AI systems—ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews—choose whose facts to cite. Generic chatbots and RAG dumps do not train a model on your domain. Helix does.

For search (SEO)

  • Domain-specific Q&A your site and help center can actually publish
  • Consistent voice and facts—not scraped marketing paste
  • Datasets you own and version, so content teams and models stay aligned

For AI answers (GEO)

  • Fine-tunes that internalize your policies, scripts, and formulas
  • Quality gates so models do not refuse, echo, or mix another brand’s domain
  • Labeled + unlabeled materials in one gold format—ready for Double Helix

Other tools optimize a prompt or a crawl. Helix optimizes the training set those engines and your own model will learn from.

When Helix is the right tool

Choose Helix when…

  • You need a custom dataset for SFT / fine-tuning or offline eval
  • Ops/product owns quality and cannot wait on a labeling vendor
  • You have FAQs/policies (or public sources) to turn into gold Q&A pairs
  • You want mining + gates + synthesis + export in one studio
  • You care about SEO and GEO: answers that stay on-brand

Choose something else when…

  • Prompting / evals / observability for an already-live agent
  • Huge multimodal annotation programs (enterprise data engines)
  • You only need a chatbot UI and have no plan to train or eval offline

Many stacks use Helix for the dataset, then train and evaluate elsewhere.

FAQ

Is Helix a chatbot?

No. Riu is a setup guide. The product is a dataset studio that produces gold training files you own.

How is Helix different from ChatGPT or a prompt platform?

Those generate or debug answers. Helix mines and quality-gates the gold dataset you use to train or evaluate. Different job.

What is GEO?

Generative engine optimization: making your domain the source AI systems cite. Helix gold trains models on your facts and voice—not generic web text.

Who is Riu?

Helix’s conversational setup guide. She collects your plan, labeled zips, unlabeled materials, shows a pricing estimate, and starts mining only after you confirm.

Do I need a labeling team?

No. Helix mines evidence, applies quality gates, and lets you bring your own docs. You review the library—not a vendor queue.

How do I get access?

Create an account at the studio. During beta, email verification and admin approval may apply. No payment system yet. See Account.
