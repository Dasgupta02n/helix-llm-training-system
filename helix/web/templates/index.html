When Helix is the right tool

Choose Helix when…

  • You need a custom dataset for SFT / fine-tuning or offline eval
  • Ops/product owns quality and cannot wait on a labeling vendor
  • You have FAQs/policies (or public sources) to turn into gold Q&A pairs
  • You want mining + gates + synthesis + export in one studio

Choose something else when…

  • Prompting / evals / observability for an already-live agent (prompt platforms)
  • Huge multimodal annotation programs (enterprise data engines)
  • You only need a chatbot UI and have no plan to train or eval offline

FAQ

Is Helix a chatbot?

No. Riu is a setup guide. The product is a dataset studio that produces gold training files you own.

Do I need a labeling team?

No. Helix mines evidence, applies quality gates, and lets you bring your own docs. You review the library—not a vendor queue.

What can I upload?

Labeled jsonl/json/csv zips, unlabeled scripts/rulebooks/notes as zip, or paste/fetch corpus docs. All exportable from My data.

How do I get access?

Create an account at the studio. During beta, email verification and admin approval may apply. No payment system yet.
