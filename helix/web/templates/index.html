Read together, all eight describe one absence: a general model has no durable, inspectable representation of the role it is being asked to hold. Producing one is the entire purpose of the system below.

Two strands, one artefact

Diagram 01
Strand one — data

Decisions, recorded correctly

Mined from your sources and gated until what remains is a set of correct calls with the reasoning attached — the raw material for reinforcing real decision-making skill.

  • Sources you nominate
  • Gated for correctness and scope
  • Deduplicated, then varied deliberately
Strand two — assimilation

Written into the weights

C7X-IO fine-tunes an open base model on that library until the behaviour is a property of the model rather than an instruction sitting in front of it.

  • Settings chosen for the library
  • Held-back examples mark the result
  • Version pinned on completion
Result — the artefact

A worker for one role

Either strand alone is inert: data with nothing to inhabit, or a capable model with no idea what your job is. Paired, they produce something deployable and accountable.

  • Weights you hold and pin
  • Boundaries it will not cross
  • A log of every decision made

Capabilities and what each one buys you

01 — 12
01

Riu, the setup interview

Riu is the only way a plan gets created. She interviews you in plain English about the role, the domain, the categories of work, the tone, the edge cases and the review bar, and turns the answers into a specification the rest of the system runs against.

Why it matters

The person who knows the job specifies it, without needing anyone to translate it into configuration. Boundaries are captured before any data is collected.

02

Plan scoping

Each plan is bound to one domain and one role. Material that falls outside that scope is rejected during mining rather than absorbed.

Why it matters

A worker trained on one role behaves predictably in it; one trained on a mixture behaves like a generalist again.

03

Web and corpus mining

Candidate material is gathered from public sources, from a corpus you supply, or both, then normalised into the same decision-and-reasoning format.

Why it matters

You can begin without a curated dataset and add your own material later without restarting the plan.

04

Hard quality gates

Refusal boilerplate, restatements, off-domain material, and answers that do not take the decision are rejected rather than scored down.

Why it matters

A model trained on hedging learns to hedge. Gating for genuine decisions reinforces real decision-making skill.

05

Deduplication

Near-identical examples are dropped, including cases that differ only in wording.

Why it matters

Coverage is what improves the worker; volume alone does not.

06

Data synthesis

Controlled variants around a correct situation — different phrasing, difficulty, and awkward cases — then re-gated.

Why it matters

The same judgment has to hold across varied situations. Synthesis produces that variation deliberately.

07

A gold library that persists

Everything that passes is kept, versioned, and reviewable. The library outlives any single training run.

Why it matters

When a better base model appears, you retrain against the same library rather than starting again.

08

Export you own

The library exports as chat-format JSONL, ready for a trainer. It is a plain file, not an API you keep paying to read.

Why it matters

Train with C7X-IO, or take the file and train it yourself. The asset still belongs to you either way.

09

C7X-IO fine-tuning

Point a library at a training job. Open Apache-2.0 and MIT instruct models up to 30B are supported. Held-back examples mark the result.

Why it matters

This is the second strand: the step that turns a dataset into a worker.

10

Self-hosted deployment

The finished worker ships as weights with instructions for standard local serving. It runs on your GPU, in your private cloud, or fully air-gapped.

Why it matters

There is no egress to review and no residency question to answer.

11

Decision logging

Each decision is recorded with the reason the worker gave and the model version that produced it.

Why it matters

You can answer why a call was made, on what basis, and under which version.

12

Retraining as the role changes

Add to the library, re-gate, and retrain to a new pinned version. The previous version stays available.

Why it matters

Change becomes a deliberate, versioned event rather than silent drift.
