How it works

Everything the desk needs. Under one system.

Two strands, one artefact: gold decisions, then weights you pin. The person specifies the junior. The junior sits next to them.

Riu
interview

Riu is the only way a plan gets created. The person who knows the job specifies the role, one perfect example, and the edge cases — including when to hand off.

Gold
library

Candidates are mined and gated. What remains is a set of correct calls with reasoning attached. Synthetics stay in a separate file.

C7X-IO
train

The library is written into a QLoRA adapter on an Apache-2.0 or MIT instruct base. Version pinned. You download the zip.

Local
run

Serve it with Ollama, Open Interpreter, LM Studio, or llama.cpp on a shop PC. Inference makes no outbound call.
