Privacy Policy

Helix is an early-stage product operated at c7xai.in. This policy is written for a beta SaaS: honest about what we collect, how we use it, and where we still rely on third-party processors. It is not legal advice for every jurisdiction.

1. Who we are

Helix (“we”, “us”) is a training-data studio for building gold datasets for LLM fine-tuning and evaluation. Operator contact for privacy questions: dasgupta.02n@gmail.com (see also About and Contact).

2. What data we collect

  • Account data — email address, name (if provided), password hash, workspace membership, and admin/verification flags.
  • Workspace content you create — research plans, topic formats, corpus documents (pasted text or fetched page content), gold and synthetic examples, job settings, and related metadata.
  • Operational logs — job progress, agent run summaries, error messages, and similar product telemetry needed to run mining and show status.
  • Technical data — IP address (for rate limiting and security), browser user-agent, cookies/session tokens for authentication, and basic request metadata.
  • Email delivery metadata — if transactional email is enabled (verification, admin approval), we process recipient address and delivery status via our email provider.

3. How we use data

  • Provide and secure the Helix service (accounts, workspaces, jobs, libraries, export).
  • Run mining and synthesis workflows you start (including calls to configured gather and LLM providers).
  • Send essential product emails (e.g. verification or admin approval) when those features are enabled.
  • Improve reliability and safety (rate limits, abuse prevention, debugging failures).
  • We do not sell personal data. We do not use your private corpus or gold library for advertising.

4. Processors and third parties

Depending on configuration, Helix may send data to:

  • Hosting / database — the VPS and database where the application and your workspace data run.
  • LLM provider (e.g. OpenRouter) — prompts and evidence snippets needed to judge relevance, synthesize training pairs, or run conversational setup.
  • Gather provider (e.g. Apify) — search queries and URLs you authorize the product to fetch for discovery/evidence.
  • Email provider (e.g. Resend) — transactional messages when email is configured.

Public web gathering uses sources available on the open web. Bring-your-own corpus content stays under your account and is used to produce training examples for your workspace.

5. Retention

Gold and synthetic library rows are designed for indefinite retention in your account until you archive or delete them, or your account is removed. Job logs and operational records are kept as long as needed to operate the product and investigate issues. Beta: we may refine retention schedules as the product matures.

6. Your choices

  • Access and export your library data from the product (export tools in My data).
  • Request account or data deletion by emailing dasgupta.02n@gmail.com.
  • Do not paste secrets (API keys, passwords, personal data of others) into corpus or plans unless you intend that content to be processed by Helix and its configured providers.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures appropriate for an early product (hashed passwords, session tokens, rate limits, HTTPS at the edge when configured). See Security. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; beta users should avoid highly regulated or sensitive personal data until a formal compliance program is published.

8. Children

Helix is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as Helix evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above and, when practical, a notice in the app.
