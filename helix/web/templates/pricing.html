How we design cost (so you can audit us)

  • Gold with your sources: about $0.75–$1.00 per row.
  • Gold with no sources (after the 10 + 10 review): about $2–$3 per row.
  • Synthetics: about $0.04–$0.20 per row, stored separately.
  • Jobs: auto-pause if spend trajectory would exceed that ratio; you must confirm to continue past the cap.
  • In-app meter: model and gather shown separately, using billed amounts when available.
  • Double Helix v1 (not charged by Helix): QLoRA on an Apache-2.0 or MIT instruct model you choose (up to 30B). GPU is pay per train (idle when unused; Helix never leaves a machine running). You can download your gold and train anywhere, or confirm a train here — Helix fetches gold from your account and then gives you a zip of the adapter + tokenizer. Designed around $15–50 per train job.

What you’re paying for (when paid plans land)

Helix is a training-data studio: plain-English plans, evidence mining, gold quality gates, synthesis, and export—not a hosted 30B model and not a prompt/eval console. When paid tiers launch, we will publish clear limits (gold targets, batch volume, seats) here before charging.

Fair use during beta

  • No scraping for illegal content or clear ToS abuse of third-party sites.
  • No multi-account abuse to bypass rate limits.
  • We may reset demo data or adjust free quotas as the product stabilizes.
