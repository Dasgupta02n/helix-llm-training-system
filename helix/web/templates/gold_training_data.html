The direct answer

Most teams who want a custom LLM are handed one of five tools: a hosted chat box, a prompt debugger, a labeling factory, a synthetic-data script, or a train button that assumes the dataset already exists. C7X starts one step earlier. Riu interviews the person who knows the role, then C7X mines evidence, quality-gates gold examples, keeps synthetics in a separate file, and exports a library you can train anywhere — or train here with C7X-IO.

What C7X is for

  • A support, refund, hiring, or ops role that must behave the same way tomorrow.
  • A team that has the job in their head, not a finished JSONL file.
  • A buyer who wants gold examples with sources, not a prompt that disappears on refresh.
  • A deployment that must run on hardware you control, with weights you can pin.

The path (seven steps)

  1. Role. What job will this model do?
  2. One perfect example. Input, ideal output, why it is correct.
  3. Edge cases. High-risk roles need more of them. C7X will not skip this.
  4. Your files, or none. Labeled zip, unlabeled materials, or public evidence.
  5. Mine and gate. Candidates are gathered, then accepted or rejected. No partial credit.
  6. Library. Gold and synthetics stored apart, downloadable as separate files in one zip.
  7. Export or train. Take the zip. Or confirm C7X-IO and download a QLoRA adapter.

Frequently asked

What is a gold training-data studio?

Software that collects verified input–output examples for one job, rejects weak rows, stores a library you own, and can train from that library. C7X is built only for that job.

How is this different from a chatbot?

A chatbot answers once. C7X produces a dataset and, if you confirm train, weights that keep answering the same way after you leave the site.

Do I need a labeling team?

No. The person who knows the role talks to Riu. You may upload files. C7X mines and gates. You review instead of staffing a floor.

Can I bring my own documents?

Yes. Labeled pairs become gold. Unlabeled materials (scripts, rulebooks, SOPs) are converted into trainable rows. You can also start with no corpus and a 10-example review.

Who owns the export?

You do. Gold, synthetics, and any trained adapter zip download to your account. C7X does not host your production model.

Where does inference run after train?

On hardware you choose. C7X-IO returns an adapter to load beside an Apache-2.0 or MIT instruct base you already have a license to use.
