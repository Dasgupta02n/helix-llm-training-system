Service status

Helix is an early beta. We do not publish a 99.9% uptime number or a status-vendor widget. If the homepage and studio load, the product is up.

What “up” means here

  • Public pages (this site) are static HTML served with the app.
  • The studio at /app needs the API and database.
  • Mining and training jobs keep running after you sign out; they can still fail if a helper or GPU job errors.

If something is down

Email dasgupta.02n@gmail.com with the time, the page or job id, and what you saw. We do not have an on-call SLA during beta.
