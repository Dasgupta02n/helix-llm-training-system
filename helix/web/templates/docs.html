Documentation · Product guide

Helix docs

Helix is a no-code gold training-data studio. You define a domain plan, mine or paste evidence, quality-gate gold Q&A pairs, optionally synthesize variants, and export for fine-tuning or evaluation. This page is the public product guide (not the OpenAPI explorer).

When to use Helix

Use Helix when you need a task-specific dataset (support replies, HR handbook Q&A, sales objections, social brand voice pairs, etc.) and you want product/ops teams to drive quality— without standing up a labeling army.

Prefer prompting + eval tools (e.g. prompt versioning / live review) when you are iterating on an already-deployed assistant and are not ready to fine-tune. Prefer enterprise labeling platforms when you need huge multimodal annotation programs. Helix owns the wedge: fast domain dataset mining + gold gates + export.

Core concepts

Plan

The research plan (domain, mission, categories, sources, tone) steers mining and quality. Change the plan when you switch domains (e.g. food-delivery support → HR policy)—ontology and discovery target the active plan.

Riu

Conversational setup: product, quality bar, labeled gold zip, then unlabeled materials (scripts, rulebooks, formulas). Riu shows a pricing estimate and starts mining only after you type start.

Formats / topics

Example formats define what a good training row looks like (input/output shape). They set the quality bar for gold promotion.

Mining

Jobs gather evidence (web search when configured), score relevance by domain, and promote candidates. Quality modes trade helper depth vs cost. Jobs continue after logout. Combined spend is capped at about $35 per 1,000 gold (scaled to job size); jobs pause for consent if trajectory would exceed the cap.

Corpus (bring your own)

Paste FAQ/policy text or fetch a URL. Corpus is promoted into evidence and gold before generic web scraps when you mine—critical for niche or internal knowledge public search will not find.

Your labeled data

Upload a zip of jsonl/json/csv with input+output (or question/answer). Stored as gold-format rows, downloadable from My data · Export my uploads.

Unlabeled materials

Upload scripts, rulebooks, lesson notes as a zip. Helix converts them into trainable gold-format pairs (Export materials). They sit alongside mined gold and labeled uploads for later Double Helix training.

Gold library

Verified training pairs live in your account library indefinitely. Rejected rows keep rejection reasons. Seed/demo rows are labeled separately. Collection goals count verified rows only.

Synthesis

Create controlled variations of gold (tone, persona, difficulty, etc.) for volume while preserving business logic.

Export

Download gold/synthetic sets and train anywhere. Or start Double Helix: Helix fetches gold from your account, trains QLoRA on pay-per-run GPU, then gives you a zip of the adapter, tokenizer, gold used, and load_adapter.py (not a full merged 30B dump). Downloading the trained zip requires accepting an ownership/liability declaration; a copy is emailed and kept even if you delete the account.

Quick start

  1. Open the studio and create/sign into a workspace.
  2. Talk to Riu to set the role, a perfect example, edge cases, and the plan (domain + categories + mission).
  3. Optionally paste a corpus FAQ under My data.
  4. Start a mining job; watch live progress; review gold with origin badges.
  5. Export when the library meets your goal.

Architecture (short)

  • Gather — web search or your corpus, never invented by the LLM.
  • Judge / synthesize — configured language model with hard quality gates.
  • Store — multi-tenant Postgres-backed libraries and job state.

API

The interactive app uses a versioned HTTP API under /api/… (auth, workspaces, library, jobs, Riu). OpenAPI UI may be disabled in production; use the product UI as the source of truth for workflows, or contact us for partner API access.
